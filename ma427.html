Supplemental Course Information.

Introduction

This course initiates a two-semester sequence in numerical analysis (continued by MA 428), emphasizing the theory, error analysis, and computer implementation of practical algorithms for nonlinear problems: approximation of functions by interpolating polynomials, numerical differentiation and integration, and the numerical solution of ordinary differential equations. (MA 427 is cross-listed as CSC 427.)

Programming exercises in Matlab are woven throughout the course so that students can put the theory learned in class into practice.

  • Prerequisite: MA 341 or MA 301, and one of CSC 111, CSC 112, CSC 113, CSC 114, CSC 116, MA 116, PY 251, ST 114, or ECE 209. A working knowledge of programming is expected.

Homework

Individual assignments will be given roughly every 1-2 weeks, with written theory problem sets and Matlab programming exercises tied to the current topic. Each assignment is submitted as a single PDF on Moodle.

Submission format (please read carefully):

  • The programming parts (which typically require plotting) must be typed up in LaTeX and submitted as a PDF, with your code and figures included. Handwritten programming write-ups will not be accepted.

  • The theory parts may be handwritten, provided the writing is neat and legible and the scan is clear. Typing them in LaTeX is strongly recommended (and excellent practice for communicating mathematics).

Presentation bonus: a homework that is written and presented nicely  —  clear, well-organized, and professionally typeset  —  will receive one extra point.

Using LaTeX
LaTeX can help produce beautifully typeset documents and is particularly useful for communicating mathematics.

Late submissions are not acceptable.
